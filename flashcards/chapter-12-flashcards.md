## Chapter 12: Full Exam Simulation and Time-Management Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | AT-420 exam time budget per question (50 questions, 90 minutes)? | ~108 seconds (1.8 min) per question. Exam tip: use 100-110 sec/question as your working pace target, not an exact rule for every item. |
| 2 | AT-420 passing score requirement? | 70% = 35 out of 50 correct. Exam tip: memorize the raw number (35), not just the percentage, for quick mental tracking during the exam. |
| 3 | Checkpoint pace at the 45-minute mark? | You should have attempted ~25 of 50 questions. Exam tip: check your question counter against the clock at this exact halfway point to catch pacing drift early. |
| 4 | How much time should you reserve at the end for review? | 5-10 minutes. Exam tip: this buffer is for answering any remaining flagged questions, not for re-reading questions you already feel confident about. |
| 5 | Best action when a question takes longer than ~60-90 seconds and you're unsure? | Select your best-guess answer, flag it, and move on. Exam tip: never leave a question blank — blanks are always scored as incorrect. |
| 6 | How should you prioritize last-minute review across domains? | Prioritize by (exam weight) x (your score gap to mastery) — biggest weighted gap first. Exam tip: a weak score in a low-weight domain is lower priority than a weak score in a high-weight domain. |
| 7 | Flagging vs. skipping a question — what's the difference? | Flagging keeps your selected answer and marks it for revisit; skipping with no answer selected scores as wrong. Exam tip: always pick an answer before flagging. |
| 8 | Equal-time pacing vs. checkpoint pacing — which is recommended? | Checkpoint pacing (verify progress at set intervals, e.g. every 25 questions) is recommended over rigid equal-time-per-question pacing, since question difficulty varies. |
| 9 | Common distractor pattern across all exam domains? | An answer that is technically correct but incomplete — it ignores a stated constraint (cost, scale, security, compliance). Exam tip: always re-check the answer against EVERY requirement in the scenario, not just the main one. |
| 10 | 33/50 on a practice exam — pass or fail? | Fail. 33/50 = 66%, below the 70% (35/50) threshold. Exam tip: always convert your raw score to the pass/fail number (35), not just a percentage, to judge readiness quickly. |
| 11 | What should you do in the final reserved review window (last 5-10 min)? | Answer any remaining flagged/blank questions first, then re-check only your lowest-confidence flagged answers if time remains. Exam tip: never re-open answered-and-confident questions — this wastes time and can introduce second-guessing errors. |
| 12 | Why never leave a question unanswered on AT-420? | Unanswered questions are always scored as incorrect — there is no penalty difference between a wrong guess and a blank. Exam tip: always select an answer before time runs out, even a guess. |
