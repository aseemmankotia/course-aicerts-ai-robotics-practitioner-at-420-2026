# Chapter 7 Lab: NLP for Human-Robot Interaction

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Spec an LLM-driven voice-control stack for a robot arm, including ASR engine, intent parser, action-grounding layer, and safety validator, with a sample command-to-action mapping

## Key takeaway

Natural language control of robots requires a full pipeline — ASR, NLU, grounding, and safety validation — not just an LLM alone.
