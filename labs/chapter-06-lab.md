# Chapter 6 Lab: Generative AI for Robotic Creativity

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Spec a generative-AI-assisted design workflow for creating synthetic training scenarios for a pick-and-place robot, listing inputs, model type, and validation checks

## Key takeaway

Generative AI accelerates robot design and data creation, but every output destined for hardware needs a human or simulation validation gate.
