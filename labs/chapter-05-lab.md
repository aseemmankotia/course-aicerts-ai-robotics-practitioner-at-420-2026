# Chapter 5 Lab: Reinforcement Learning for Robotics

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Design a sim-to-real RL pipeline plan for a quadruped locomotion task, specifying simulator, domain randomization parameters, and real-world validation steps

## Key takeaway

RL adds power for complex locomotion and manipulation tasks, but reward design and the sim-to-real gap are the exam's favorite traps.
