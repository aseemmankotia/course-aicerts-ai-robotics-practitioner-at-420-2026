## Chapter 12 Cheat Sheet: Exam Simulation & Time Management

### Core Numbers
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total questions | 50 |
| Total time | 90 minutes |
| Time per question (budget) | ~108 sec (~1.8 min) |
| Passing score | 70% = 35/50 |
| Recommended final review buffer | 5-10 minutes |
| Checkpoint: by 45 min elapsed | ~25 questions attempted |
| Checkpoint: by 70 min elapsed | ~39 questions attempted |

### Pacing Rule
If a question takes longer than ~90 seconds without a confident answer: **pick your best guess, flag it, move on.** Never leave a blank — blanks always score as wrong, guesses have a chance.

### Flag vs. Skip
| Action | Answer Saved? | Scored if time runs out? |
|---|---|---|
| Flag + answer | Yes | Counts as your answer |
| Skip (no answer) | No | Always incorrect |

### Domain-Weighted Review Priority (last-minute review)
Priority score = **Exam Weight % × Score Gap to Mastery**
- High weight + low practice score → review FIRST
- Low weight + high practice score → review LAST (or skip)
- Never spend equal time across domains regardless of weight — this wastes high-value review time.

### End-of-Exam Review Order
1. Answer every remaining blank/flagged question first (guarantees no zero-credit blanks).
2. With leftover time, revisit only your LOWEST-confidence flagged answers.
3. Do not reopen questions you answered with confidence — this risks introducing errors without expected gain.

### Common Distractor Patterns (seen across all domains)
- **Technically correct but incomplete**: satisfies part of the scenario but misses a stated cost/scale/security constraint. (Most commonly missed trap.)
- **Deprecated or wrong-service option**: sounds plausible but refers to outdated or mismatched tooling.
- **Overcomplicated solution**: uses more services/steps than needed — exams penalize unnecessary complexity when a simpler compliant answer exists.
- **Partially-right numeric value**: close to a real limit/threshold but off by one tier or order of magnitude.

### Score Interpretation
| Raw Score | Percentage | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 35/50 | 70% | Pass (minimum) |
| 33/50 | 66% | Fail |
| 40/50 | 80% | Pass (strong margin) |

Always convert a practice score to the raw number needed (35) rather than relying on percentage alone — faster to track mentally mid-exam.
